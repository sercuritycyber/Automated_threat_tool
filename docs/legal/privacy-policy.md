## Why we are allowed to use it (lawful basis)

- **Performance of a contract** (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) — for creating your
  account and delivering the alerts you have paid for.
- **Consent** (Article 6(1)(a)) — we use a double opt-in and record the time, IP
  address and version of these terms you agreed to. You can withdraw consent at
  any time using the unsubscribe link in every message or your preference centre;
  withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before you withdrew it.
- **Legitimate interests** (Article 6(1)(f)) — keeping minimal security and abuse
  logs, and defending legal claims. We have balanced this against your rights.

We do not carry out any automated decision-making that produces legal or
similarly significant effects, and we do not use your data for advertising or
sell it to anyone.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: access a copy of your data;
have inaccurate data corrected; have your data erased; restrict or object to
processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent.

- Use the **"download my data"** and **"delete my account"** buttons in your
  preference centre, or
- email {{contact_email}}.

We will respond within one month. There is no charge unless a request is
manifestly unfounded or excessive.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk),
helpline 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

## Security
