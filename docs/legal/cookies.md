# Cookies

**Last updated:** {{terms_version}}

The {{brand}} website at {{site}} does **not** set any cookies and does not use
analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party advertising scripts.

The only state we keep about you is your account record in our database, created
when you subscribe. Preference and unsubscribe links are signed tokens in the
URL, not cookies.

## Payment pages

When you go to pay, you are redirected to **Stripe Checkout**, hosted on Stripe's
own domain. Stripe sets its own cookies there, strictly to process the payment
and prevent fraud. See Stripe's cookie and privacy notices at
[stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy). You come back to our site once
payment is done.

## Your chosen channels

If you connect Slack or Telegram, those apps operate under their own cookie and
privacy policies when you use them.
