**Financial Viability & UK Tax**

* **The £1,000 Trading Allowance:** As a UK sole trader, HMRC grants you a £1,000 tax-free trading allowance. You can test the market and onboard your first batch of subscribers (e.g., 10 users at £8/month = £960/year) without needing to pay tax on that trading income or even formally register for Self Assessment if it remains your only side income.
* **Monzo Business Advantage:** You can leverage Monzo Business's built-in Making Tax Digital (MTD) tax tool. Powered by Sage, this tool categorises transactions and tracks income and expenses in real-time. This eliminates the need to juggle manual spreadsheets and automatically prepares you for MTD compliance.
* **Payment Processing:** Connect a payment gateway like Stripe or Lemon Squeezy to your Monzo account to handle the recurring weekly or monthly subscriber payments autonomously.

**Legal & Compliance**

* **ICO Registration:** If your business processes personal data—which includes running a mailing list of customer email addresses—you are legally required to pay the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) data protection fee. For a Tier 1 micro-business, this mandatory annual fee ranges between £40 and £52. As a bonus for a cybersecurity brand, paying the fee places you on the public ICO register, demonstrating accountability and robust data-protection governance.
* **Liability Limitations:** You will need clear terms and conditions stating that your service aggregates public advisories for informational purposes only. This protects you legally if a severe vulnerability bypasses your RSS heuristics.
